## Entry requirements

Each row must include:

- a public repository;
- a public link showing a Kepenk policy, workflow, hook, adapter, or case study;
- the integration type;
- maintainer consent;
- the verification date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.

Stars, forks, downloads, and unsupported statements are not adopter evidence.

Use the adopter pull-request template to add an entry. A maintainer may request removal through an issue or pull request. See [the adoption guide](docs/adoption.md), [the case-study template](docs/case-study-template.md), and [the Ustaca AI founding-team case study](docs/case-studies/ustaca-ai.md).
